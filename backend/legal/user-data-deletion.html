User Data Deletion

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Data retained for registered users

For registered users, GoodLang retains only the server data needed to operate the account and service. This may include the account email, service usage history, practice scores, completion time, grammar rule codes, and analytical or technical metadata. We do not store the full text of user answers.

Requesting deletion

For any inquiry about deleting your GoodLang user data, contact goodlang.app@gmail.com and provide the email address associated with your user account.

Non-registered users

Data belonging to non-registered users is not retained on the GoodLang server.
